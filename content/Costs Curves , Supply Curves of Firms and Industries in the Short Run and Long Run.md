### SHORT RUN :

By short run we mean some factor of production is constant. (eg. take the capital constant.) 
#### 1. Firms :

Firms don't respond to markets directly. Firms have the data of their own sales and at most they are aware about the market environment amidst the other firms. A Firm responds to the "demand faced by the firm" and that demand is altered by the market changes.

In the determination of firm's decisions whether to stay in business or not, this demand faced by the firm plays a key role.

But firstly, let's be clear about the shapes of the cost curves.
ATC curve will be determined by the shapes of AVC and AFC. 
AFC curve's shape is a decreasing curve, to be precise a rectangular hyperbola, as AFC.y=constant .
Shape of AVC can be swiftly determined using calculus, but that will require some real work in here. But as usual we have an economic way of interpretation to find the explanations/results in economics, here we will use law of variable proportions :
###### Law of variable proportions: 
It states that in short run, the output of firm operates in three stages. First output increases increase in input, with  increasing additional output that we get from additional unit of input i.e. with increasing marginal productivity. Then in second stage the output still increases with increase in input but the additional output that we get from additional unit of input is decreasing, i.e. marginal productivity decreasing, until it reaches its maximum output. In the third stage, the output starts to decrease, and the marginal productivity becomes negative i.e. the additional output is negative, when we put another input unit , which for example means output is getting reduced  by employing more labour.

Now , in general setting with only labour as factor of production , 
	                 $$VC = w.L \quad \implies AVC = \frac{w}{APL}$$ 
where APL = average product of labour, denoted by AP in the graph.
so we will get a U shaped variable average cost curve. 
In the same spirit it can be proved, 
             $$VC = w.L \quad \implies MC = MVC =  \frac{w}{MPL}$$
 
where MPL = average product of labour, denoted by AP in the graph.
so the MC curve will also be a U shaped curve with minimum below the AVC curve.
